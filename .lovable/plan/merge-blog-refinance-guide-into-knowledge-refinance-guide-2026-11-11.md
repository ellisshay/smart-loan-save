# Merge /blog/refinance-guide into /knowledge/refinance-guide

## What changes for visitors
- The guide at /knowledge/refinance-guide gets the two things only the blog post had, written in plain Hebrew with no dashes: a short "השלבים במחזור משכנתא" section and the "מתי כדאי למחזר" situations it lacks.
- Anyone opening /blog/refinance-guide goes straight to the guide.
- The blog post no longer appears in the blog list, the sitemap or the pre-built pages.

## 1. Content merged into the guide (src/data/knowledge/articles/refinance.ts)
The guide already covers: what refinancing is, internal vs external, the early repayment fee and its parts, the worked example, the pre-decision checklist and "lower payment vs save interest". From the blog post it is missing only:
- **The step-by-step process:** check current terms (rates, balances, exit dates), get the fee breakdown from the bank in writing, get offers from several banks, compare total saving against all costs, then go ahead with the best offer. This goes in as a new ordered list after "מה בודקים לפני שמחליטים", merged with that checklist so nothing repeats.
- **When it's worth it:** one more situation, "tracks taken when rates were high". It joins the existing paragraph in "מה זה מחזור משכנתא" as a short list.
- **Dropped from the blog:** the "800,000 / 1% / 400 to 600 ₪" saving claim (it has no source, and the guide already has a worked example marked «דוגמה»), the "3 to 5 years" rule of thumb, "at least 3 banks" (it becomes "כמה בנקים") and the "המדריך המלא ל־2026" wording.
- updatedDate is set to 2026-11-11. Title, slug, H1, canonical, Article schema and breadcrumbs stay as they are.
- All new text uses «משכנתא» and «מחזור», with no dashes.

## 2. Redirect (src/seo/pages.ts)
Add `"/blog/refinance-guide": "/knowledge/refinance-guide"` to `redirects`. The existing setup then handles it: the router redirect, plus a pre-built stub with a canonical to the guide, noindex,follow and an instant refresh.

## 3. Stop the blog page competing
- `src/entry-server.tsx` `getPublicRoutes`: filter out any path that is a key in `redirects`, so the stub is never overwritten and the sitemap skips it.
- `src/pages/BlogPage.tsx`: remove the refinance-guide card.
- `src/pages/ArticlePage.tsx`: remove the refinance-guide entry from `expandedGuides`.
- The blog article's data entry in `articlesData.ts` stays in place (no content deletion). It is simply never routed to.

## 4. Links
- In `articlesData.ts`, the 3 posts with `relatedSlugs` containing "refinance-guide" (lines ~217, 256, 326) have it removed from `relatedSlugs`. A small `relatedGuides` map in ArticlePage adds a related card pointing to /knowledge/refinance-guide (with the guide's title) for those 3 posts. Related cards will never render `/blog/refinance-guide`.
- A repo-wide search found no other hard-coded `/blog/refinance-guide` links. The build check below confirms this again.

## 5. Sitemap
public/sitemap.xml is regenerated by the build and lists only /knowledge/refinance-guide.

## 6. Checks before reporting
